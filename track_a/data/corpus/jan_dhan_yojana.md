# Jan Dhan Yojana — Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

## Overview
PMJDY is India's flagship financial inclusion scheme launched in August 2014. It provides universal access to banking facilities with at least one basic bank account for every household.

## Key Benefits
- **Zero balance bank account** with no minimum balance requirement.
- **RuPay debit card** with inbuilt accident insurance cover of ₹2 lakh.
- **Overdraft facility** of up to ₹10,000 for one year (after 6 months of satisfactory operation).
- **Life insurance cover** of ₹30,000 for eligible account holders.
- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) from government schemes into the account.
- Mobile banking facility available.

## Eligibility
- Any **resident Indian citizen** aged 10 years or above.
- No requirement for minimum income or documents.
- Simplified KYC: Aadhaar card is sufficient for opening the account.
- One account per person.

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card** (primary document — sufficient for account opening).
- If Aadhaar is not available: any government-issued photo ID (Voter ID, PAN card, Passport, Driving License).
- Passport-size photograph.
- Minor applicants (10-18 years) need a parent/guardian's Aadhaar.

## How to Apply
1. Visit any **bank branch** or **Business Correspondent (BC) point** near you.
2. Fill out the **PMJDY application form** (available in regional languages).
3. Submit Aadhaar card or alternative ID proof.
4. Account is opened immediately.
5. Collect your RuPay debit card from the bank within 7-10 days.

## Important Contacts
- **PMJDY Toll-Free**: 14477 / 1800-11-0001
- **Website**: pmjdy.gov.in
- **Check account status**: Visit your bank branch or use the BHIM UPI app.

## Notes
- Over 53 crore Jan Dhan accounts have been opened since 2014.
- Total deposits in PMJDY accounts exceed ₹2 lakh crore.
- The scheme is a key enabler for DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) under other welfare schemes like PM-KISAN, MGNREGA wages, and Ujjwala subsidy.
