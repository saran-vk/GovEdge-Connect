# Ayushman Bharat — Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)

**Official Authority:** National Health Authority (NHA), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India  
**Corpus Version:** 1.0  
**Source Portal:** https://pmjay.gov.in/ and https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest government-funded health assurance scheme. Launched in September 2018, it aims to protect vulnerable low-income families from catastrophic health expenditures and medical debt. The scheme provides completely cashless and paperless access to secondary and tertiary healthcare services at public and empanelled private hospitals across India.

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
Eligibility under PM-JAY is entitlement-based, derived primarily from the Socio-Economic and Caste Census (SECC 2011) database for rural and urban areas, plus enrolled Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana (RSBY) families.

### Rural Eligibility Criteria (SECC Deprivation Categories):
A rural household is eligible if it falls under any of the following deprivation categories:
- **D1:** Only one room with kuccha walls and kuccha roof.
- **D2:** No adult member between ages 16 and 59.
- **D3:** Female-headed households with no adult male member aged 16 to 59.
- **D4:** Households with disabled members and no able-bodied adult member.
- **D5:** SC/ST households.
- **D7:** Landless households deriving a major part of their income from manual casual labor.
- **Automatically Included:** Destitute, living on alms, manual scavenger households, primitive tribal groups, and legally released bonded laborers.

### Urban Eligibility (11 Defined Occupational Categories):
Ragpickers, beggars, domestic workers, street vendors, cobblers, construction workers, plumbers, masons, painters, security guards, sanitation workers, transport workers (drivers, conductors, cart pullers), shop workers, and artisans.

### Senior Citizens (70+ Expansion):
All senior citizens aged 70 years and above, irrespective of their socio-economic status or income, are eligible for an additional Rs. 5,00,000 annual health cover under the expanded PM-JAY.

## 3. Documents Required
To verify eligibility and generate an Ayushman Card (Golden Card):
1. **Aadhaar Card:** Mandatory for biometric e-KYC authentication.
2. **Family Identification Proof:** Ration Card, PMJAY family letter (containing HHID / Family ID), or State NFSA beneficiary database ID.
3. **Active Mobile Number:** For OTP verification during e-KYC.

---

## 4. Application and Card Generation Process
PM-JAY has no enrollment fee or subscription process; beneficiaries are automatically identified.
1. **Verify Eligibility:**
   - Online: Visit [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in) or use the Ayushman App. Enter mobile number, select state, scheme (PMJAY), and search by Aadhaar number, Ration card number, or Family ID.
   - Offline: Visit nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or check with the local Panchayat / ASHA worker.
2. **Ayushman Card Generation:**
   - Complete e-KYC using Aadhaar OTP, IRIS scan, or fingerprint biometrics.
   - Capture a live photograph.
   - Upon verification approval by the nodal authority, download the Ayushman Card (PVC/e-card).
3. **Hospital Admission (Ayushman Mitra Desk):**
   - Approach the 'Ayushman Mitra' help desk at any empanelled public or private hospital.
   - Present the Ayushman Card or Aadhaar card for instant verification and pre-authorization.

---

## 5. Benefit Amount & Coverage Structure
- **Coverage Amount:** Cashless health cover of up to **Rs. 5,00,000/-** per eligible family per year.
- **Family Size:** No restriction on family size, age, or gender. All eligible family members are covered under the single floater entitlement.
- **Scope of Coverage:** Covers secondary care (medical/surgical procedures) and tertiary care (specialized surgeries, oncology, cardiology, neurosurgery, knee replacement, etc.) across 1,949+ medical procedures.
- **Pre & Post Hospitalization:** Covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalization consultations/diagnostics and up to 15 days of post-hospitalization medication and follow-up care.
- **Pre-existing Conditions:** All pre-existing medical conditions are covered from day one of enrollment without waiting periods.
- **Portability:** Benefits are national and fully portable across any empanelled hospital in India.

---

## 6. High-End Specialty Treatment Packages
PM-JAY covers tertiary interventions across 1,949 pre-notified health benefit packages:
- **Cardiology & Cardiothoracic Surgery:** Angioplasty with drug-eluting stents, coronary artery bypass graft (CABG), valve replacements, and pacemaker implantations.
- **Oncology (Cancer Treatment):** Comprehensive surgical oncology, chemotherapy cycles, immunotherapy, and linear accelerator radiotherapy.
- **Nephrology & Urology:** Hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, renal stone interventions, and kidney transplant surgical packages.
- **Orthopedics & Polytrauma:** Hip and knee joint replacements, spinal decompression and fixation, and emergency intensive care polytrauma management.

---

## 7. National Portability & Multi-Hospital Treatment
- **Interstate Healthcare Access:** Beneficiaries can seek cashless treatment at any empanelled hospital (public or private) anywhere in India without requiring prior State transfer certificates or referrals.
- **Empanelled Network:** Covers over 28,000+ public and private hospitals across the country connected via the National Health Authority Transaction Management System (TMS).
- **Ayushman Mitra Assistance:** Present at hospital admission desks 24/7 to facilitate patient e-KYC, doctor allocation, package selection, and discharge authorization.

---

## 8. Grievance Helpline (14555) & Anti-Fraud Guidelines
- **Toll-Free National Helpline:** Citizens can dial **14555** or **1800-111-565** (24/7 toll-free) for eligibility checks, empanelled hospital searches, and filing grievances.
- **Zero Out-of-Pocket Cost Rule:** Empanelled hospitals are strictly forbidden from charging patients for medicines, consumables, bed charges, or diagnostic tests covered under the authorized package.
- **National Anti-Fraud Unit (NAFU):** Monitors abnormal claim spikes, phantom surgeries, and illegal co-payments. Patients can report fraud or demands for bribes directly through the grievance portal (grievance.pmjay.gov.in) or district health authorities.
