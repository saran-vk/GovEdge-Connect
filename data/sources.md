## 1. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Official Portal:** https://pmkisan.gov.in/
- **Local Guideline Corpus:** [data/schemes/pm_kisan.md](file:///home/saran/Projects/S5_mini_antiG/data/schemes/pm_kisan.md)
- **Authority:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Retrieved Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Key Intents Covered:**
  - `check_eligibility`: Landholding definitions, institutional and tax-payer exclusion criteria.
  - `documents_needed`: Mandatory Aadhaar, Khasra/Khatauni land records, DBT-enabled bank account.
  - `apply_process`: Online self-registration via Farmer Corner, CSC centers, State Agriculture Nodal Officers.
  - `benefit_amount`: Rs. 6,000 per year paid in three equal 4-month installments of Rs. 2,000 via DBT.

## 2. MGNREGA (Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act)
- **Official Portal:** https://nrega.nic.in/
- **Local Guideline Corpus:** [data/schemes/mgnrega.md](file:///home/saran/Projects/S5_mini_antiG/data/schemes/mgnrega.md)
- **Authority:** Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India
- **Retrieved Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Key Intents Covered:**
  - `check_eligibility`: Rural adult household members willing to undertake unskilled manual labor.
  - `documents_needed`: Identity proof, residence certificate, photographs, post office/bank passbook.
  - `apply_process`: Gram Panchayat application, free Job Card issuance within 15 days, work demand receipt.
  - `benefit_amount`: Guaranteed minimum 100 days of employment per household, state statutory wage rates, unemployment allowance.

## 3. Ayushman Bharat (AB-PMJAY)
- **Official Portal:** https://pmjay.gov.in/ & https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/
- **Local Guideline Corpus:** [data/schemes/ayushman_bharat.md](file:///home/saran/Projects/S5_mini_antiG/data/schemes/ayushman_bharat.md)
- **Authority:** National Health Authority (NHA), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
- **Retrieved Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Key Intents Covered:**
  - `check_eligibility`: SECC 2011 rural deprivation (D1-D5, D7), 11 urban worker categories, senior citizens 70+.
  - `documents_needed`: Aadhaar card for biometric e-KYC, Ration Card / Family ID, mobile number.
  - `apply_process`: Portal verification, Ayushman Mitra kiosk at empanelled hospital, CSC PVC card issuance.
  - `benefit_amount`: Up to Rs. 5,00,000 cashless health coverage per family per year for secondary/tertiary care without family size cap.
  - `operational`: High-end tertiary surgical packages, national portability, anti-fraud helpline 14555.

## 4. Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)
- **Official Portal:** https://pmayg.nic.in/
- **Local Guideline Corpus:** [data/schemes/pmay_g.md](file:///home/saran/Projects/S5_mini_antiG/data/schemes/pmay_g.md)
- **Authority:** Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India
- **Retrieved Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Key Intents Covered:**
  - `check_eligibility`: Homeless and kutcha house households via SECC 2011 / Awaas+, 13-point exclusion filter.
  - `documents_needed`: Aadhaar card, bank/post office passbook, MGNREGA job card number, land possession certificate (Patta).
  - `apply_process`: Automated PWL selection, BDO administrative sanction, AwaasApp geo-tagged photo inspections across 4 construction stages.
  - `benefit_amount`: Rs. 1,20,000 (plain) / Rs. 1,30,000 (hilly/NE) unit grant, 90–95 days MGNREGA wages, Rs. 12,000 SBM toilet grant, free Ujjwala LPG connection, optional Rs. 70,000 subsidized bank loan.
