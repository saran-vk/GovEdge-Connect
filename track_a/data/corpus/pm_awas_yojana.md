# PM Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) Rural Housing

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) is a central scheme that provides
financial assistance to eligible rural households for the construction of a
pucca house. The scheme aims to provide housing to every eligible household by
offering a grant plus subsidised labour, so beneficiaries are not required to
take a loan for the basic house.

## Eligibility criteria
- The beneficiary household must be identified through the SECC 2011 list for
  housing deprivation parameters.
- Preference is given to households without any shelter, households with
  zero, one, or two rooms with kutcha walls and kutcha roof, and households
  belonging to SC, ST, and minority communities.
- Priority is also given to households having a disabled member, landless
  households, and families living below the poverty line.
- The house must be built on land owned by the beneficiary; the scheme also
  assists with the Awaas+ survey for left-out eligible families.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance of up to Rs. 1.20 lakh in plain areas and up to
  Rs. 1.30 lakh in hilly or difficult areas for house construction.
- Additional assistance for toilets under Swachh Bharat Mission.
- Support of 90 to 95 person-days of unskilled labour under MGNREGA.
- Assistance for convergence with LPG connection, electricity, and drinking
  water facilities.

## Required documents
- Aadhaar card of the beneficiary.
- SECC 2011 eligibility details / Awaas+ survey record.
- Land ownership proof (patta / khata).
- Bank account details and Aadhaar seeding.

## Application and status
- Applications are submitted through the gram panchayat and the Awaas+ mobile
  application.
- Status of the house and instalments can be tracked on the PMAY-G dashboard
  using the beneficiary Aadhaar number.
