# Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

**Official Authority:** Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India  
**Corpus Version:** 1.0  
**Source Portal:** https://pmayg.nic.in/

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) is the flagship rural housing scheme launched in November 2016 to realize the goal of "Housing for All" in rural India. The primary objective is to provide financial assistance to homeless rural households and those living in dilapidated, unserviceable kutcha houses to build a pucca house with basic amenities including power, clean cooking gas, drinking water, and sanitation.

---

## 2. Eligibility & Beneficiary Selection Criteria
Beneficiaries under PMAY-G are identified using housing deprivation parameters from the Socio-Economic and Caste Census (SECC 2011) database, updated and saturated via the Awaas+ survey:
- **Eligible Households:** Houseless families and rural households living in zero, one, or two-room houses with kutcha walls and kutcha roofs.
- **Priority Ranking:** Priority is given to vulnerable social groups including SC/ST households, primitive tribal groups, single women heads of households, families with disabled members, and landless manual laborers.
- **Exclusion Criteria (13-Point Filter):**
  - Ownership of motorized 2/3/4 wheelers, fishing boats, or mechanized agricultural equipment (tractors).
  - Kisan Credit Card (KCC) with credit limit of Rs. 50,000/- or higher.
  - Any household member who is a government employee (central/state/PSU).
  - Any household member paying income tax or professional tax.
  - Ownership of 2.5 acres or more of irrigated land with irrigation equipment, or 5 acres or more of unirrigated land.
- **Gram Sabha Social Audit:** Beneficiary lists generated from the Permanent Wait List (PWL) must be verified and approved by the local Gram Sabha.

---

## 3. Documents Required
To register and verify a PMAY-G housing sanction:
1. **Aadhaar Card:** Mandatory for all adult household members for biometric verification and consent.
2. **Bank or Post Office Passbook:** Active savings account linked to Aadhaar and mapped with NPCI/DBT.
3. **MGNREGA Job Card Number:** Mandatory to map wage entitlement under MGNREGA convergence.
4. **Land Ownership or Land Possession Certificate (Patta):** Document demonstrating ownership or legitimate possession of homestead land. (For landless families, the State Government provides residential land).
5. **Mobile Number:** Registered for SMS alerts and geo-tagging notifications via AwaasApp.

---

## 4. Application, Geotagging & Construction Process
Beneficiaries do not need to submit physical paper applications or pay intermediaries; selection is automated from the verified PWL/Awaas+ waitlists:
1. **Sanction Order Issuance:** The Block Development Officer (BDO) issues an administrative sanction order mapped to the beneficiary's unique PMAY ID.
2. **AwaasApp Mobile Geotagging:**
   - Village Functionaries or ASHA workers use the official 'AwaasApp' mobile application to capture time-stamped and geo-tagged live photographs of the construction site across 4 inspection stages:
     - Stage 1: Existing kutcha house / vacant land plot.
     - Stage 2: Plinth level / foundation completion.
     - Stage 3: Lintel level / roof cast.
     - Stage 4: Completed pucca house with operational toilet.
3. **DBT Fund Installments:** Each installment is released directly to the beneficiary's bank account within 7 days of verified geo-tagged photo upload.
4. **House Specifications:** Minimum carpet area of **25 square meters** (exclusive of toilet), designed for seismic and environmental safety, with a dedicated area for hygienic cooking.

---

## 5. Benefit Amount & Convergence Structure
- **Unit Financial Assistance:**
  - **Plain Areas:** **Rs. 1,20,000/-** per house unit (cost shared 60:40 between Central and State Governments).
  - **Hilly, Difficult, North-Eastern States, and Himalayan/IAP Districts:** **Rs. 1,30,000/-** per house unit (cost shared 90:10).
- **MGNREGA Wage Convergence:** Beneficiaries are legally entitled to **90 to 95 person-days** of unskilled manual labor under MGNREGA (providing approximately Rs. 22,000 to Rs. 30,000 in additional wage support) to assist in building their own home.
- **Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM-G) Toilet Grant:** Additional assistance of **Rs. 12,000/-** for constructing an attached hygienic household toilet.
- **Clean Energy Convergence:** Automatic fast-track linkage to **Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)** for a free LPG cooking gas connection.
- **Optional Institutional Bank Loan:** Beneficiaries can avail of an optional institutional bank loan of up to **Rs. 70,000/-** at a subsidized interest rate with 3% interest subvention.

---

## 6. Installment Disbursement & Geo-Tagging Milestones
The total unit assistance is credited directly into the beneficiary's bank account via PFMS in 3 sequential installments based on construction progress:
- **First Installment (Advance / Foundation: ~₹40,000):** Released immediately upon administrative sanction and site preparation so the beneficiary can procure foundation materials.
- **Second Installment (Roof-Cast / Lintel Level: ~₹60,000):** Released once walls and roof casting are completed and verified through time-stamped AwaasApp geo-tagged photos.
- **Third Installment (Completion & Finishing: ~₹20,000):** Released upon full completion of plastering, doors, windows, hygienic cooking alcove, and functional toilet verified on AwaasSoft.
- **Fund Transfer Order (FTO):** Monitored digitally without physical cheques or intermediary interference.

---

## 7. Rural Mason Training (RMT) & Disaster-Resilient Construction
- **Certified Skill Development:** Integrates the Rural Mason Training program under the National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF).
- **Disaster-Resilient Typologies:** Masons are trained in indigenous, climate-appropriate, earthquake-resistant, and flood-resilient house designs customized for each agro-climatic zone.
- **Livelihood Convergence:** Trainee masons earn standard daily wages under MGNREGA during hands-on house construction sessions.

---

## 8. Landless Beneficiaries & PM-JANMAN Convergence
- **Land Allocation Mandate:** For eligible rural families identified in SECC/Awaas+ who do not own homestead land, State Governments must provide residential land or financial grants for land purchase prior to issuing house sanctions.
- **PM-JANMAN Mission Integration:** Fast-track housing saturation for Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs) residing in deep forest fringe and hilly habitations.
- **Composite Utilities:** Houses must be integrated with Jal Jeevan Mission for potable tap water and Saubhagya for household electricity.

---

## 9. Grievance Redressal & Public Tracking
- **Online Beneficiary Tracking:** Beneficiaries can inspect their FTO generation dates, credited installment sums, and uploaded inspection photographs on `pmayg.nic.in` using their Registration Number.
- **Appellate Structure:** Complaints regarding wrongful omission from the Permanent Wait List, bribe demands, or sub-standard materials can be filed with the Block Development Officer (BDO) or District Collector.
- **Gram Sabha Oversight:** Mandatory social audits conducted twice a year by the Gram Sabha to review all sanctioned houses and ensure zero corruption.
