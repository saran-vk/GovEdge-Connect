# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

## Overview
PM-KISAN is a central government scheme that provides income support of
Rs. 6,000 per year to all landholding farmer families across India. The amount
is paid in three equal instalments of Rs. 2,000 every four months, directly
into the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account through Direct Benefit
Transfer (DBT).

## Eligibility criteria
- Applicant must be a citizen of India and a landholding farmer.
- All landholding farmer families having cultivable land in their name are
  eligible, irrespective of the size of the landholding.
- Institutional landholders and farmer families with a serving or retired
  gazetted officer, a serving or retired officer of PSU or government
  autonomous bodies, and persons who paid income tax in the last assessment
  year are excluded.
- Pensioners with a monthly pension of Rs. 10,000 or more and professionals
  like doctors, engineers, and lawyers who pay income tax are also excluded.

## Benefits
- Rs. 6,000 per year per eligible family, paid as Rs. 2,000 every four months.
- Direct cash transfer into the Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
- No repayment is required; it is a direct income support transfer.

## Required documents
- Aadhaar card.
- Land ownership records / land records (khasra / khatauni).
- Bank account details linked to Aadhaar.
- Proof of being a farmer, e.g., crop or land cultivation records.

## Application and status
- Applications are processed through the PM-KISAN portal by the state
  government revenue department.
- Status can be checked on the PM-KISAN portal using the Aadhaar number or
  bank account number.
- The PM-KISAN helpline is 155261 / 011-24300606.
