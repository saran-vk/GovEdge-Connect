# Ujjwala Yojana — Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

## Overview
PMUY provides free LPG connections to women from Below Poverty Line (BPL) households. The scheme aims to replace unhealthy cooking fuels (firewood, coal, dung, coke) with clean LPG, improving the health of women and children.

## Key Benefits
- **Free LPG connection** to eligible BPL women.
- First refill cylinder provided free of cost.
- Security deposit for the LPG connection waived.
- Pressure regulator, hose, and domestic gas stove provided.

## Eligibility
- Woman applicant must be from a **BPL household** (identified under SECC 2011 data).
- Applicant must be **18 years or above**.
- No other LPG connection in the household.
- The household must not already have an LPG connection.
- Priority given to SC/ST households, Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana (Gramin) beneficiaries, antyodaya families, forest dwellers, and beneficiaries under Tribal Sub-Plan.

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card** of the woman applicant.
- **BPL certificate** or proof of BPL status.
- **Ration Card** (if available).
- Bank/Post Office account details.
- Passport-size photograph.
- Identity proof of all adult family members (optional but recommended).

## How to Apply
1. Visit the nearest **LPG distributor** or Common Service Centre (CSC).
2. Fill out the **PMUY application form**.
3. Submit required documents.
4. The LPG connection will be issued within 7-10 working days.
5. Contact your LPG distributor for the first refill.

## Important Contacts
- **PMUY Helpline**: 1800-233-3555 (toll-free)
- **LPG Helpline**: 1800-267-7777
- **Website**: pmuy.gov.in

## Notes
- The scheme was launched in May 2016.
- Over 10 crore LPG connections have been distributed since inception.
- Under the "Ujjwala 2.0" phase, the scheme was expanded to cover all beneficiaries of PM-KISAN, MGNREGA, and other government schemes.
