# Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY)

## Overview
Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY) is the world's
largest health assurance scheme, providing a health cover of Rs. 5 lakh per
family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation. The cover is
cashless and paperless at empanelled public and private hospitals.

## Eligibility criteria
- The scheme is meant for poor and vulnerable families identified by the
  Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 database.
- Eligible households include those with no able-bodied adult between 16 and
  59 years, households with only one room, kachha houses, households without
  any adult member between 16 and 59, and landless households deriving income
  from casual labour.
- Families with any member aged below 5 years, or a disabled member, are
  prioritised.
- The benefit extends to all members of an eligible family with no cap on
  family size or age of members.

## Benefits
- Health cover of Rs. 5 lakh per family per year.
- Covers pre-hospitalisation and post-hospitalisation expenses.
- Includes all pre-existing diseases from day one of the policy.
- Cashless treatment at any empanelled public or private hospital.

## Required documents
- Aadhaar card (where available).
- Ration card or SECC 2011 eligibility certificate.
- PMJAY golden card / e-card generated after verification.
- Identity proof such as voter ID or driving licence.

## Application and status
- Beneficiaries are identified automatically from the SECC 2011 database.
- The golden card can be downloaded from the National Health Authority portal.
- The Ayushman Bharat helpline is 14555.
