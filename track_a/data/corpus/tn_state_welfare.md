# Tamil Nadu State Welfare Schemes (Kalaignar / Chief Minister's Schemes)

## Overview
The Government of Tamil Nadu runs several state welfare schemes that run
alongside central schemes. This fact sheet covers the Kalaignar Magalir Urimai
Thogai (women's assistance), the Chief Minister's health insurance, and the
Kalaignar housing scheme for rural and urban households.

## Eligibility criteria
- Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai: women heads of family in Tamil Nadu with
  an annual family income below Rs. 2.5 lakh are eligible for Rs. 1,000 per
  month assistance.
- Chief Minister's health insurance: families identified in the state health
  insurance database with no eligible member paying income tax; the cover is
  Rs. 5 lakh per family per year.
- Kalaignar housing scheme: rural and urban families owning a house site with
  no pucca house, and families on the waiting list from the earlier
  Indira Awaas Yojana, are eligible for financial assistance for house
  construction.

## Benefits
- Rs. 1,000 per month cash transfer for eligible women heads of family.
- Cashless hospitalisation cover of Rs. 5 lakh per family per year under the
  Chief Minister's health insurance.
- Housing assistance of Rs. 2.5 lakh for rural and Rs. 4 lakh for urban
  eligible families under the Kalaignar housing scheme.

## Required documents
- Aadhaar card.
- Family card / ration card of Tamil Nadu.
- Income certificate issued by the village administrative officer.
- Land or house-site ownership documents for the housing scheme.

## Application and status
- Women's assistance applications are submitted at the nearest e-Sevai centre
  or through the Kalaignar portal.
- Health insurance enrolment is done at the district health office.
- Housing scheme allotment status can be checked at the district rural
  development office.
