# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

## Overview
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a government-backed savings scheme for the girl child, launched under the Beti Bachao Beti Padhao campaign. It provides a high interest rate and tax benefits to encourage parents to save for their daughter's education and marriage expenses.

## Key Benefits
- **High interest rate**: Currently 8.2% per annum (compounded annually) — among the highest for any small savings scheme.
- **Tax benefits**: Contributions qualify for deduction under Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 lakh). Interest earned and maturity amount are also tax-free (EEE status).
- **Government-backed**: Fully guaranteed by the Government of India.
- Partial withdrawal allowed after the girl turns 18 (up to 50% of the balance).
- Maturity after 21 years from the date of opening.

## Eligibility
- Girl child must be **below 10 years** of age at the time of account opening.
- A maximum of **two accounts per family** (one for each girl child).
- The account must be in the name of the girl child, operated by the parent/guardian.
- Indian resident only (non-residents are not eligible).

## Documents Required
- **Birth certificate** of the girl child.
- **Aadhaar card** of the girl child (or parent/guardian if Aadhaar is not available).
- **Address proof** of the parent/guardian (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.).
- Passport-size photograph.
- Minimum initial deposit: ₹250.

## How to Apply
1. Visit any **post office** or **authorized bank branch** (SBI, nationalized banks, etc.).
2. Fill out the **SSY account opening form**.
3. Submit required documents (birth certificate, Aadhaar, address proof).
4. Make the initial deposit (minimum ₹250, maximum ₹1.5 lakh per year).
5. Account becomes operational immediately.

## Important Contacts
- **India Post**: indiapost.gov.in
- **Check interest rates**: rbi.org.in or your bank branch
- **DBT status**: Check via the Sukanya Samriddhi Account portal or your bank.

## Notes
- Account matures when the girl turns 21 years old.
- Premature closure is allowed only in exceptional cases (death of the girl child, or medical emergency).
- The scheme is available at all post offices and 28 designated nationalized banks.
- Interest rate is revised quarterly by the Government of India.
