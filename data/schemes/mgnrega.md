# Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)

**Official Authority:** Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India  
**Corpus Version:** 1.0  
**Source Portal:** https://nrega.nic.in/

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
MGNREGA is a legal guarantee of wage employment in rural areas enacted in 2005. Its primary objective is to enhance the livelihood security of households in rural areas by providing at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work. Secondary objectives include creating durable rural community assets (water harvesting, rural roads, flood control) and rejuvenating natural resources.

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Rural Residency:** The applicant must be a resident of a rural area falling under the jurisdiction of the Gram Panchayat.
2. **Age Requirement:** Adult members of the rural household (at least 18 years of age) who are willing to undertake unskilled manual physical work.
3. **Household Unit:** The guarantee of 100 days of employment is per rural household, not per individual person.
4. **No Asset Disqualification:** There is no restriction on landholding or poverty line criteria; any adult rural citizen willing to perform manual labor is entitled to apply.

---

## 3. Documents Required
To apply for an MGNREGA Job Card, the applicant household must submit:
1. **Proof of Identity:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID card, Ration card, or Kisan Passbook.
2. **Proof of Residence:** Ration card or Gram Panchayat residence certificate.
3. **Photographs:** Passport-size photographs of all adult members applying for work.
4. **Bank or Post Office Account Details:** Joint or individual bank/post office savings account passbook of the adult applicants (linked with Aadhaar for DBT wage payments).

---

## 4. Application and Registration Process
1. **Application for Job Card:**
   - Adult members of a rural household submit an application (written on plain paper or prescribed format, or orally made) to the Gram Panchayat or Programme Officer.
   - The application must contain the names, ages, and genders of adult household members willing to work.
2. **Verification & Job Card Issuance:**
   - The Gram Panchayat verifies local residency and age of the applicants within 15 days of receiving the application.
   - A Job Card is issued free of cost within 15 days of application. It remains valid for 5 years and is renewable.
### Demand for Work & Worksite Distance Rules
- Job Card holders submit a written application for work to the Gram Panchayat or Ward member, indicating the dates and duration (minimum 14 continuous days).
- A dated receipt must be issued to the applicant confirming receipt of the work application.
- Employment must be provided within 15 days of application or from the date employment is sought, whichever is later, within a 5 km radius of the village. If work is provided beyond 5 km, an additional 10% wage is payable to cover travel costs.

---

## 5. Benefit Amount & Wage Disbursement Structure
- **Guaranteed Employment:** Minimum 100 days of wage employment per financial year per registered rural household. (Can be extended up to 150 days in notified drought/natural disaster affected areas).
- **Daily Wage Rates:** Notified state-wise annually by the Central Government under Section 6(1) of the Act (ranging typically between Rs. 230 to Rs. 380 per day depending on the state). Equal wages are mandated for both men and women.
- **Payment Method:** Electronic fund transfer (NeFMS/DBT) directly into the post office or bank accounts of the workers based on electronic muster rolls (e-Muster). Wages must be disbursed within 15 days of work completion.
- **Unemployment Allowance:** If an applicant is not provided employment within 15 days of submitting a valid demand for work, the State Government is legally bound to pay an Unemployment Allowance (not less than 1/4th of the wage rate for the first 30 days, and not less than 1/2 of the wage rate thereafter).

---

## 6. Permissible Works List (Schedule I Categories)
Permissible works under MGNREGA are classified into 4 core categories focusing on natural resource rejuvenation and sustainable rural livelihoods:
1. **Category A: Natural Resource Management (NRM):** Water conservation and harvesting (check dams, percolation tanks, underground dykes), watershed management, afforestation, plantation, and land development on common lands.
2. **Category B: Individual Beneficiary Works (Vulnerable Households):** Creation of productive assets on land owned by SC/ST households, small/marginal farmers, and PMAY-G beneficiaries (e.g., dug wells, farm ponds, vermi-compost pits, cattle sheds, goat shelters).
3. **Category C: Common Rural Sanitation & Infrastructure:** Construction of Anganwadi centers, village grain storage structures, solid/liquid waste management soak pits, and community sanitary complexes.
4. **Category D: Rural Connectivity:** Construction of all-weather gravel/paved roads, culverts, and causeways to connect isolated habitations to main highways.

---

## 7. National Mobile Monitoring System (NMMS) & Attendance
- **Digital Muster Attendance:** The Ministry of Rural Development mandates tracking worksite attendance through the National Mobile Monitoring System (NMMS) mobile app.
- **Geo-Tagged Photographs:** Worksite Mates must capture two time-stamped and geo-tagged live photographs of workers per day (morning check-in and afternoon work progress).
- **Offline Mode Capability:** In remote rural areas with intermittent cellular connectivity, the app operates in offline mode, syncing attendance records once network is detected.
- **Electronic Muster Rolls (e-MR):** Replaces physical paper muster rolls, preventing ghost beneficiary claims and ensuring wage calculation transparency.

---

## 8. Worksite Facilities & Wage Delay Compensation
- **Statutory Worksite Amenities (Schedule II):** Every active worksite must provide safe drinking water, temporary shade tents for rest and infant care, a certified first-aid medical box with bandages and antiseptics, and crèche facilities if more than 5 children under age six accompany working mothers.
- **Worksite Mate Supervision:** A designated female mate or disabled worker acts as supervisor to ensure amenities and accurate task measurement.
- **Wage Delay Compensation Mandate:** Wages must be paid within 15 days of muster roll closure. If wages are delayed past 15 days, workers are legally entitled to delay compensation of 0.05% of the unpaid wage per day of delay, automatically calculated by NREGASoft.
