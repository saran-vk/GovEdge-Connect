# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

**Official Authority:** Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India  
**Corpus Version:** 1.0  
**Source Portal:** https://pmkisan.gov.in/

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a Central Sector Scheme with 100% funding from Government of India. The scheme provides income support to all landholding farmer families across the country to enable them to take care of expenses related to agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs.

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- All landholding farmer families having cultivable land in their names in the respective State/UT land records are eligible.
- A "farmer family" is defined as a family comprising husband, wife, and minor children who own cultivable land as per land records of the concerned State/UT.

### Exclusions:
The following categories of beneficiaries are NOT eligible for benefits under PM-KISAN:
1. Institutional landholders.
2. Farmer families in which one or more members belong to the following categories:
   - Former and present holders of constitutional posts.
   - Former and present Ministers, State Ministers, Members of Parliament, Members of Legislative Assemblies/Councils, Mayors of Municipal Corporations, and Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
   - All serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government Ministries, Departments, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), and autonomous institutions (excluding Class IV/Multi-Tasking Staff/Group D employees).
   - All superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is Rs. 10,000/- or more (excluding Class IV/Group D employees).
   - All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
   - Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with professional bodies and carrying out practice.

---

## 3. Documents Required
To apply for PM-KISAN, the applicant must have:
1. **Aadhaar Card:** Mandatory for all beneficiaries.
2. **Land Ownership Proof:** Khasra/Khatauni/land revenue record verifying cultivable landholding in the applicant's name.
3. **Bank Account Details:** Active bank account linked (seeded) with Aadhaar and enabled for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT/NPCI mapping).
4. **Mobile Number:** Linked to Aadhaar for OTP verification and SMS notifications.

---

## 4. Application and Registration Process
Eligible farmers can enroll through the following modes:
1. **Self-Registration via Portal:**
   - Visit the official portal [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in).
   - Navigate to 'Farmers Corner' -> 'New Farmer Registration'.
   - Select Rural or Urban farmer, enter Aadhaar number, mobile number, and select the State.
   - Enter OTP received on the Aadhaar-linked mobile, fill land details (survey number, khasra, area), and upload ownership documents.
2. **Common Service Centres (CSCs):**
   - Farmers can visit the nearest village CSC with their Aadhaar and land records. The Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE) assists in uploading data on payment of standard nominal service charges.
3. **Local Revenue / Agriculture Nodal Officer:**
   - Submit the physical application form with verified revenue documents to the local Patwari, Lekhpal, or designated State Nodal Officer.

---

## 5. Benefit Amount & Disbursement Structure
- **Total Annual Benefit:** Rs. 6,000/- per eligible farmer family per year.
- **Disbursement Mode:** Transferred directly into the bank accounts of beneficiaries through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
- **Payment Schedule:** Divided into three equal installments of Rs. 2,000/- each:
  - 1st Installment: April – July period
  - 2nd Installment: August – November period
  - 3rd Installment: December – March period
- **Mandatory Compliance:** Beneficiaries must complete e-KYC (biometric via CSC or facial/OTP via PM-KISAN app) to continue receiving installments.
