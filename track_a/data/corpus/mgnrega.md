# MGNREGA — Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act

## Overview
MGNREGA is a flagship employment guarantee scheme by the Government of India. It guarantees 100 days of wage employment per year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work.

## Key Benefits
- **100 days** of guaranteed wage employment per year per household.
- Minimum wage paid as per the State Government notification.
- Work provided within 5 km of the applicant's residence.
- Unemployment allowance if work is not provided within 15 days of application.
- Compensation of 0.05% per day for delayed wage payments.

## Eligibility
- Must be a member of a rural household.
- Adult members (18 years or above) of the household who volunteer for unskilled manual work.
- Any rural household in India — no income or caste restriction.
- Job Card must be issued by the local Gram Panchayat.

## Documents Required
- **Job Card** issued by the Gram Panchayat.
- **Aadhaar Card** of the applicant.
- **Bank/Post Office account details** for direct benefit transfer (DBT).
- **BPL certificate** (if applicable).
- Proof of rural residence (ration card, voter ID, etc.).

## How to Apply
1. Visit the local **Gram Panchayat** office.
2. Apply for a **MGNREGA Job Card** with required documents.
3. Once issued, demand work by submitting an application to the Gram Panchayat.
4. Work must be provided within 15 days; if not, apply for unemployment allowance.

## Important Contacts
- **MGNREGA Helpline**: 1800-180-1551 (toll-free)
- **Website**: nrega.nic.in
- **Wage payment status**: Check via the MGNREGA portal or mobile app.

## Notes
- MGNREGA also covers convergence with other schemes like PMAY-G for housing construction.
- Women workers are entitled to equal wages as men.
- The scheme prioritizes SC/ST households and women-headed households.
